FOLIO-2025 SHADER STUDY AND ADAPTATION

Source: https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025
Inspected commit: 41046b57eeed8d156d9c3fd7fa259900baef7816

The GLSL material and atmosphere implementation adapts the lighting model
documented in these source files: MeshDefaultMaterial.js, Ligthing.js,
Fog.js, Materials.js, DayCycles.js and cheapDOF.js.
The car models, scene layout, shot design and music are original to this film.
